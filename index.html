Our Heritage & Passion

Bringing the Streets of Pakistan to Your Plate

DesiHut was established with one mission: to capture the aromatic magic of Pakistani street bazaars and family dawat recipes. Situated in Latifabad Unit #3 on Auto Bahn Road, we bring together Hyderabad’s rich culinary culture with the finest chaats, biryanis, and succulent savories.

From our slow-simmered Sindhi Dum Biryani and chilled sweet-savory Dahi Bhallay to our signature secret-marinated Chicking Special, each plate is made fresh to order with genuine desi ghee, roasted spices, and zesty home-churned chutneys.
